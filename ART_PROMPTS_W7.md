# World 7, stop 1 (the Hill of Hachilah, 1 Samuel 26): art prompts (11-21)

Most of the stop can use art already in Part Two: Ziph's Hachilah, Hills and Mountain (Glen's, 11-15) darkened to
night in the game, as Carmel's fields are. **Only one room needs new pictures: the Place of the Wagons**, plus kit and
clips. Save them in `david-sling-2-art/w7/`.

| Room | Art it uses | New art? |
|---|---|---|
| David's Camp in the Wilderness of Ziph | Ziph's Hills (or Carmel's camp), darkened | no |
| The Hill of Hachilah by night | Ziph's Hachilah (dusk), darkened | no |

Each outdoor room has three layers: **far** (the full picture, with sky), **mid** (the sky keyed out) and **near** (a
strip along the bottom, everything above it keyed out). The key color is **green (#00FF00)**. Every prompt is
complete, so paste one per image.

## 2. Kit (separate pieces, each on solid flat bright green #00FF00, side view, at night, moonlit)
1. `A heavy wooden ox-cart of ancient Israel about 1000 BC with two solid wooden wheels, loaded with baggage under a cloth, seen straight from the side, at night in moonlight.` (to climb onto and cross)
2. `The same kind of wooden cart tipped up on its end, its back resting on the ground, room to crawl under it, seen from the side, at night.` (to crawl under)
3. `A pile of army baggage: sacks, rolled tents, water skins and a basket, seen from the side, at night.`
4. `A small campfire burned down to glowing red embers in a ring of stones, a thin curl of smoke, seen from the side, at night.`

## 3. Clips (2-3 seconds, side view, facing right, feet on one flat line, pure #00FF00 behind, camera still)
Use your David, Abishai, Saul and Abner from the other clips, in the same clothes.
1. **Soldiers asleep:** `A soldier of King Saul's army of ancient Israel asleep on the ground wrapped in a cloak, his head on his arm, breathing slowly, then turning over in his sleep.` (Two or three different men, so a row of them doesn't repeat.)
2. **Abner asleep:** `Abner, the captain of Saul's army, a strong bearded man in armor, asleep sitting against a cart wheel, his head nodding.` (26:7)
3. **David creeping:** `...David creeping forward slowly and carefully, bent low, one hand out, looking from side to side.`
4. **David taking the spear and the jar:** `...David kneeling, carefully pulling a spear up out of the ground, then picking up a clay water jar, then standing.` (26:12, the moment)
5. **David walking with them:** `...David walking, carrying a long spear over his shoulder and a clay water jar in his other hand.`
6. **The young man for the spear:** `A young soldier of Saul's army running forward, then stopping and bending to pick up a spear from the ground.` (26:22)
7. **Saul waking:** `...King Saul waking up on the ground, sitting up, looking around in alarm for his spear.` (Optional: for the morning in the camp, before David calls.)
