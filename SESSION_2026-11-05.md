## The approach (decided with Glen)
- **Orthographic everything.** Every prompt says "orthographic side view, no perspective".
- **Kits, not paintings.** Glen makes a repeating **wall strip** plus a **kit sheet** of pieces on pure green (#00FF00).
  Claude cuts the pieces and places them in `game/region.js`.
- **The hybrid look:**
  - **Outdoors** is Super Mario World style: a far sky layer (scrolls at 0.12), a middle layer (0.45), the kit level
    on top, and foreground plants in front of David (parallax about 1.3).
  - **Indoors** is Castlevania style: the wall strip at 1:1, the kit on top, and occasional front pieces (railings).
- Paintings are only for special story or boss rooms, if ever.

## New tools
- `tools/cut_kit.py <sheet> <out> names... --scale --keep`
  - Cuts the green-screen kit pieces.
  - `--keep` drops stray sparks.
- `tools/cut_sheet.py <sheet> <out> <prefix> rows... --scale --ms --loop --even row:n --drop row:k`
  - Cuts creature sprite sheets by their rows and the blobs in each row.
  - Glen's creature sheets so far: `spider_*`, `bat2_*`, `rat2_*`. The old `rat_run` is kept for the main game.
- `tools/make_strip.py`: makes a wall strip repeat without a seam. To line a strip's floor up with the game's
  ground, shift the image first so its wall base sits at y 612.
