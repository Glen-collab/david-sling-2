# World 7: The Hill of Hachilah, then Ziklag (plan, 2026-11-21, a first draft for Glen)

Glen 11-21: "It can be like Nob and Gath. Those were one world but two stops." So World 7 is **one stage with two
stops on the land map**, built the way World 3 is (`nobgath.js`: one save, `landmap.js` has two places sharing a
`key`; the second shows once the first is done, and picking it starts in its own room):

| Stop | Chapter | Story |
|---|---|---|
| **The Hill of Hachilah** | 1 Samuel 26 | the second time David spares Saul: the spear and the jar of water |
| **Ziklag** | 1 Samuel 27; 30 | Achish gives David Ziklag; it's burned; "David strengthened himself in the LORD"; the pursuit |

Carmel ends with 25:42, Abigail riding out to become David's wife. Hachilah opens with 26:1, the Ziphites telling Saul
again. This plan is mostly the first stop; Ziklag gets its own plan when Hachilah's built.

## Stop 1: the Hill of Hachilah (a proposal: Glen decides)
**The heart of it:** En-Gedi was "I won't lift my hand against him" with Saul walking into David's cave. This time
David walks into Saul's camp, by night, on purpose, and stands over him with the spear right there. Abishai says the
obvious thing (26:8). David says no (26:9-11). The boss is a **reversed boss** again: the win is to take the spear and
the jar and not strike.

**What's different from En-Gedi:** there you hid and waited. Here you go in. **"No man saw it, or knew it, nor did any
awake; for they were all asleep, because a deep sleep from the LORD had fallen on them"** (26:12). So the camp isn't a
guard-dodging level: nobody wakes, and that's the point (the LORD did it). The challenge is getting through **the place
of the wagons** (26:5, 7): a maze of carts, baggage and three thousand sleeping men, at night, with Abishai behind you.
- Sleeping soldiers lie across the way: step over them (Up and over), crawl under the wagons, climb onto the carts and
  across. They turn over in their sleep and mumble, so a gap opens and closes. None of them wakes.
- Abishai follows you (like Jonathan or the band in other worlds). He's quick and keeps wanting to hurry ahead.
- Fires burned down to embers: little pools of light, dark between (Carmel's night, with the embers for the lights).

**The center: Saul asleep, the spear stuck in the ground at his head, the jar of water** (26:7). Abner and the people
around him (26:7). Abishai: 26:8. Then a choice (as Abigail's words): Abishai's way, or David's. Abishai's way never
happens; David answers 26:9-11 every time, and the player picks up the spear and the jar.

**The getaway, then the call across the valley** (26:13-25): out of the camp with the spear and the jar, then up "the
top of the mountain far away, a great space being between them" (26:13): a climb (the wall-climb from En-Gedi). At the
top, the cut scene: David calls to Abner (26:14-16), Saul knows his voice (26:17), David's question (26:18-20), Saul's
"I have sinned" (26:21), the spear sent back with a young man (26:22), 26:23-25. The Journal page.

**The item: Saul's water jar** (ITEMS_PLAN.md has it as World 7's). The spear goes back to Saul (26:22); Scripture
doesn't say the jar does, so David keeps it. Fill it at a spring, pour it on fire: it puts out the fires of burned
Ziklag in the second stop, and the burning thornbushes ITEMS_PLAN.md lists for older worlds.

## The story beats (1 Samuel 26, WEB from ebible.org/eng-web, checked 2026-11-21; the game says "the LORD" for "Yahweh")
- 26:1 The Ziphites came to Saul to Gibeah, saying, "Doesn't David hide himself in the hill of Hachilah, which is before the desert?"
- 26:2 Then Saul arose and went down to the wilderness of Ziph, having three thousand chosen men of Israel with him, to seek David in the wilderness of Ziph.
- 26:3 Saul encamped in the hill of Hachilah, which is before the desert, by the way. But David stayed in the wilderness, and he saw that Saul came after him into the wilderness.
- 26:4 David therefore sent out spies, and understood that Saul had certainly come.
- 26:5 Then David arose and came to the place where Saul had encamped; and David saw the place where Saul lay, with Abner the son of Ner, the captain of his army. Saul lay within the place of the wagons, and the people were encamped around him.
- 26:6 Then David answered and said to Ahimelech the Hittite, and to Abishai the son of Zeruiah, brother of Joab, saying, "Who will go down with me to Saul to the camp?" Abishai said, "I will go down with you."
- 26:7 So David and Abishai came to the people by night; and, behold, Saul lay sleeping within the place of the wagons, with his spear stuck in the ground at his head; and Abner and the people lay around him.
- 26:8 Then Abishai said to David, "God has delivered up your enemy into your hand today. Now therefore please let me strike him with the spear to the earth at one stroke, and I will not strike him the second time."
- 26:9 David said to Abishai, "Don't destroy him, for who can stretch out his hand against the LORD's anointed, and be guiltless?"
- 26:10 David said, "As the LORD lives, the LORD will strike him; or his day shall come to die, or he shall go down into battle and perish.
- 26:11 The LORD forbid that I should stretch out my hand against the LORD's anointed; but now please take the spear that is at his head and the jar of water, and let's go."
- 26:12 So David took the spear and the jar of water from Saul's head, and they went away. No man saw it, or knew it, nor did any awake; for they were all asleep, because a deep sleep from the LORD had fallen on them.
- 26:13 Then David went over to the other side, and stood on the top of the mountain far away, a great space being between them;
- 26:14 and David cried to the people, and to Abner the son of Ner, saying, "Don't you answer, Abner?" Then Abner answered, "Who are you who calls to the king?"
- 26:15 David said to Abner, "Aren't you a man? Who is like you in Israel? Why then have you not kept watch over your lord the king? For one of the people came in to destroy your lord the king.
- 26:16 This thing isn't good that you have done. As the LORD lives, you are worthy to die, because you have not kept watch over your lord, the LORD's anointed. Now see where the king's spear is, and the jar of water that was at his head."
- 26:17 Saul recognized David's voice, and said, "Is this your voice, my son David?" David said, "It is my voice, my lord, O king."
- 26:18 He said, "Why does my lord pursue his servant? For what have I done? What evil is in my hand?
- 26:19 Now therefore, please let my lord the king hear the words of his servant. If it is so that the LORD has stirred you up against me, let him accept an offering. But if it is the children of men, they are cursed before the LORD; for they have driven me out today that I shouldn't cling to the LORD's inheritance, saying, 'Go, serve other gods!' (in: Glen 11-21)
- 26:20 Now therefore, don't let my blood fall to the earth away from the presence of the LORD; for the king of Israel has come out to seek a flea, as when one hunts a partridge in the mountains."
- 26:21 Then Saul said, "I have sinned. Return, my son David; for I will no more do you harm, because my life was precious in your eyes today. Behold, I have played the fool, and have erred exceedingly."
- 26:22 David answered, "Behold the spear, O king! Let one of the young men come over and get it.
- 26:23 The LORD will give to every man his righteousness and his faithfulness; because the LORD delivered you into my hand today, and I wouldn't stretch out my hand against the LORD's anointed.
- 26:24 Behold, as your life was respected today in my eyes, so let my life be respected in the LORD's eyes, and let him deliver me out of all oppression."
- 26:25 Then Saul said to David, "You are blessed, my son David. You will both do mightily, and will surely prevail." So David went his way, and Saul returned to his place.
- Kid-clean: 26:16's "you are worthy to die" is David scolding Abner; it can stay or be cut to "This thing isn't good
  that you have done."
- The Truth: 26:23, "The LORD will give to every man his righteousness and his faithfulness."
- 26:20's partridge is already in the game (World 3's road: the partridge's verse), a nice echo.

## The Journal's song for World 7 and 8
Psalm 57's song is all used but its Outro (11-21, from the code: Gibeah Verse 1, Ramah Verse 2, Nob and Gath
Verse 3, Adullam the Chorus, Keilah the Bridge, Ziph and En-Gedi the Final Chorus's two halves, Carmel Verse 3's second
half again). Four stops are left: Hachilah, Ziklag, Hebron, Jerusalem. The idea: **the song goes on**, Glen writes new
verses, and the Outro stays for the very end (Jerusalem), when the whole song plays.

For Hachilah, **Psalm 54** fits as nothing else does: its heading is this night. "A contemplation by David, when the
Ziphites came and said to Saul, 'Isn't David hiding himself among us?'" (Psalm 54, WEB, checked 11-21):
- 54:1 Save me, God, by your name. Vindicate me in your might.
- 54:2 Hear my prayer, God. Listen to the words of my mouth.
- 54:3 For strangers have risen up against me. Violent men have sought after my soul. They haven't set God before them.
- 54:4 Behold, God is my helper. The Lord is the one who sustains my soul.
- 54:6 With a free will offering, I will sacrifice to you. I will give thanks to your name, the LORD, for it is good.
- 54:7 For he has delivered me out of all trouble. My eye has seen triumph over my enemies.
(54:5, "Destroy them in your truth," is the one David doesn't do to Saul that night.)
