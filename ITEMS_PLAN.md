# What David carries: where he gets it, what it opens, and why you go back (draft for Glen, 2026-11-14)

Glen (11-14): "I am still using all tools like shield and sword and bow and arrow. Start thinking of where I should get
these items, in what levels, and how they will help further the adventure or bring me back to other levels because I
needed them."

The rule, Castlevania-style: **every world gives one new thing**, it's needed to finish that world, and it also opens
**sealed spots in earlier worlds** (the seal system is already built: `rSeal` in region.js says "Come back with X: World N").
What's behind a seal is always worth the trip: a Truth (Journal), a stone of remembrance, a pomegranate (more health),
an oil jar, or a short cut.

Bible references are where each thing comes from in the story. Quotes get checked on ebible.org before they go in the game.

## How it plays when you go back
- The land map already lets you walk back to any place you've finished. Each place's name on the map shows a small
  count, like "Truths 3/5", so you know there's more there.
- A seal you can't open yet talks once: "Cracked stone... Something heavy could break it." With the item, the same spot
  shows a small glint so you know to try.
- Nothing needed to finish the story is behind a backtrack; only extras (the Journal, health, the gold times).
