# Glen's Gibeah play-through: running notes (2026-11-06)

Notes from Glen as he plays. "Fixed" means done this session; "Waiting" means he said don't change it yet.

## Fixed
- **Restarting Gibeah:** the pause menu's restart kept everything found. The pause menu now has "Back to your last olive press" and "Start Gibeah over" (it asks first). Erasing a game on the title screen now erases Gibeah too.
- **Philistines ran through the tower and walls:** they only checked for ground under their feet. Now:
  - they jump up onto anything up to 3 tiles tall (the boulder, the low wall) and drop off the far side;
  - at anything taller (the watchtower), or at the end of their patrol, they stop and pace behind their shield until they have a way to David again;
  - when David gets away, they walk back to their post, hopping what's in the way.

- **The Journal played the level's music under the journal harp.** The music loop kept fading the stage music back in. Now while the Journal is open (or the song plays on the card page), only that plays. The stage music comes back after it closes.

- **A campfire in the palace halls:** it's an olive press now, like every save point. (The press picture is also loaded up front, so it can't pop in late.)
  - Glen's idea for later: a stairway to a separate save room, like Castlevania's save rooms. Not built.
- **Saul's room music:**
  - Saul's dark music (Part One's `saul_dark.mp3`) plays while he's troubled, with no fields music under it.
  - It drops low while David plays the harp.
  - It's gone once he's calm, and nothing plays under the Journal but the journal harp.
- **The hidden wall in the harp room:** you had to leave and come back before it could be broken.
  - Now, once Saul is calmed and the verse (and the Journal, if it opens) closes, he fades away after about 3 seconds.
  - The wall can be struck right then.

- **The Walls rebuilt above the yard:**
  - Eight rows of walkways staggered like brickwork, in place of the three stacked columns.
  - 39 ladders between rows, using Part One's ladder art and climbing (Up / Down; A jumps off). Ladders can now hang between two walkways, not only stand on the ground.
  - More crows and ravens on the walkways, and Philistines walking the wider ones.
  - The same plank walk as the watchtower lookout, all along the top of the wall.
- **Pillars behind walkways, and the door in front of one:** the pillars are placed last now, only where no walkway, ladder, door or stone is in front of them. In practice that leaves two short ones at the bottom.
- **The watchtower seemed to have two backgrounds:** the camera moved up and down with every jump, so the whole wall slid. Now it holds still through a jump and moves to his new height once he lands, unless he nears the top or bottom of the screen. This applies to every tall room.
- **The song (Glen):** "Let's not play David's song until the very end like his first game."
  - The Journal pages, the gate's last one too, have only the journal harp, with no stage music.
  - At the gate, the harp carries on into the card.
  - The card no longer has "A: play the song". It's switched off, not deleted (`SONG_ON` in region.js), ready for the end of the game.
- **Philistines on the railings:** their feet went to the top of the tile row, not the walkway's planks. They stand on the planks now.
- **Philistines shaking into a blur:**
  - Pacing behind the tower, they flipped direction every frame once past the edge of their pacing area. Fixed.
  - Chasing, they jittered right under or over David. Now they stand still there.
  - With David up or down on another walkway, they go back to pacing their own instead of chasing.
- **Stones passed through flying ravens:** a flying bird is drawn centered 20 px below its position, but its hitbox sat above that point. The box is centered on the drawn bird now, and a little wider.

- **The Journal's found list (Glen 11-06):** the "0 of 3 remembrance stones" tally, which made it look like he'd missed things, is gone.
  - At the bottom of the left page: "Found in Gibeah:" with a line for each kind (Truths with their references, Stones of remembrance, Pomegranates, Oil jars).
  - A line appears once he's found the first of that kind, and new finds are written in the next time he writes. The list stays on every page.
  - The first stone of remembrance or the first book of truth sits him down to write right away.
  - "2 of 3" totals appear only on the gate's last page.
  - The entries and song lines still fill in a bit after each story moment.
