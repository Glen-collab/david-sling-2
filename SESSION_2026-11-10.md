## The button mash (Glen: David acting mad)
- drill.js: `startMash({ title, need, time, drain, onDone, onFail })`. A "MADNESS" bar (like a life bar) that A and B fill (each press adds 1/need),
  drains on its own, and has to be full before the clock runs out; too slow -> "They see through it!" -> onFail (none given: it starts over).
  Two players both mash into the one bar. `mash.level` (0-1) is for the scene (David's stagger/scribble). Knobs: `MASH` at the top of it.
- Default: 40 presses to fill, 7 s, drain 0.10/s -> about 68 presses in 7 s (~10 a second): hard with one button, doable alternating A and B.
- Test: tools/tests/mash.js (55 presses in 5 s fills it; leaving it alone fails). Not wired to a Gath room yet (World 3 isn't built).
