# World 4 art prompts: cave backdrops shaped to the rooms (2026-11-13)

Why: Glen liked the pictures smaller, but they were 16:9 (or 4:1 strips) shown half size, so each one only covers the
bottom half of the screen and part of the room. The game fills the gaps by mirroring the picture side by side and repeating
its top strip above it, fading to dark. That's the "layered on top of each other" look.

The fix is pictures shaped like the rooms, painted as if seen from far back, so everything in them is already small.

## Sizes (screen 1280x720; the picture scrolls at 0.35 of David's speed)
- **Flat rooms: 3:1 wide** (3072x1024 or bigger). One picture then covers the whole room, floor to roof, with no repeats.
  If the generator can't do 3:1, make 2:1 and give the same prompt again with "continue this cave to the right"; we stitch them.
- **Bat Roost, Crumbling Ledges: 1:2 tall** (1024x2048).
- **Old Well Shaft: 1:3 tall** (1024x3072), or two 1:2 pictures, top half and bottom half.

## The prompt (fill in the [room])
> Ultra-wide 3:1 panorama of [room], an ancient limestone cave in Judea, seen from far back with a wide-angle lens so the
> whole chamber fits in frame, from the floor to the roof. Everything looks small and far away: tunnel mouths are about a
> sixth of the picture's height. Stylized 3D animated-film look, soft painterly lighting, rich detail. The floor is a flat,
> plain strip along the bottom edge with nothing in front of it. No people, no animals, no large rocks or ledges in the
> foreground. Even lighting from left to right, and the left and right edges match so the picture can repeat seamlessly.

For the tall rooms, change the first line to "Tall 1:2 vertical view up a cave shaft ..." and "from the floor to the opening
at the top".

The ledges David stands on stay separate (the kit pieces), so the pictures should have no ledges that look walkable.
