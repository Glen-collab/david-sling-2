# "My Heart Is Steadfast" (Glen, from Psalm 57)

Glen's song for Part Two's Journal, like "The Shepherd Leads Me" in Part One.
Audio: `assets/audio/my_heart_is_steadfast_full.mp3` (5:43; a copy is in `david-sling-2-art/audio/`).
Received 2026-11-05. It will be wired to the Journal pages with the character card.

```
[Intro – Solo Ancient Hebrew Harp]
[Gentle, delicate harp picking, slow and reflective]

[Verse 1]
Be merciful to me, O God
Be merciful to me
My soul takes refuge in You
My shelter, Lord, are Your wings

Until the storm has passed away
Until the danger disappears
Beneath the shadow of Your wings
I will find refuge here

[Verse 2]
I cry out to God Most High
The God who watches over me
He sends His mercy from the heavens
His truth will set me free

Though lions gather all around
And sharpened words are aimed at me
Your loving kindness reaches heaven
Your faithfulness will carry me

[Chorus]
Be exalted, O God, above the heavens
Let Your glory fill the earth
Be exalted, O God, above the heavens
Let all creation know Your worth

My heart is steadfast, O my God
My heart is steadfast in You
I will sing, I will praise Your name
For Your mercy carries me through

[Instrumental – Solo Harp]
[David plays a beautiful, flowing ancient harp melody, gradually becoming more confident]

[Verse 3]
They laid a net before my feet
They dug a pit to bring me down
But those who plotted against me
Will fall into their own ground

My heart is steadfast, O my God
My heart is steadfast still
Though I am hiding in this cave
I will trust Your perfect will

[Chorus]
Be exalted, O God, above the heavens
Let Your glory fill the earth
Be exalted, O God, above the heavens
Let all creation know Your worth

My heart is steadfast, O my God
My heart is steadfast in You
I will sing, I will praise Your name
For Your mercy carries me through

[Bridge – Quiet, Almost Whispered]
Awake, my glory, awake
Awake, my harp, awake
I will awaken the dawn
I will praise You, Lord

Among the nations I will sing
Among the people praise Your name
Your loving kindness reaches heaven
Your truth will never change

[Final Chorus – Stronger, Emotional]
Be exalted, O God, above the heavens
Let Your glory fill the earth
Be exalted, O God, above the heavens
Let all creation know Your worth

My heart is steadfast, O my God
My heart is steadfast in You
I will sing, I will praise Your name
For Your mercy carries me through

[Outro – Solo Harp]
[Slowly return to the opening melody]
Your mercy reaches to the heavens
Your truth reaches to the skies
Be exalted, O God, above the heavens
Let Your glory fill the earth

[One final harp note, fading into silence]
```

## How it could map onto the eight worlds (a first idea, to settle with Glen)
One part per world's Journal page, following the story:
1. Gibeah: Verse 1 ("Be merciful to me... beneath the shadow of Your wings")
2. Gibeah by night, Ramah: Verse 2 ("I cry out to God Most High")
3. Nob, Gath: "Though lions gather all around / And sharpened words are aimed at me"
4. Adullam: Verse 3 ("Though I am hiding in this cave", the cave of Psalm 57's heading)
5. En-Gedi: the Chorus ("My heart is steadfast")
6. Carmel and Maon: the Bridge ("Awake, my glory... I will awaken the dawn")
7. Ziklag and the Negev: "Among the nations I will sing"
8. Hebron, Jerusalem: the Final Chorus and the Outro
