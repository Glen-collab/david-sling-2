# World 6: Carmel (plan, 2026-11-20, a first draft for Glen)

Comes after En-Gedi on the land map (`landmap.js` already has Carmel as World 6, after "engedi"). En-Gedi ends with
24:22, "Saul went home, but David and his men went up to the stronghold." Carmel opens with 25:1, Samuel's death.

The world has two halves, as DESIGN.md has it: **David guards Nabal's flocks**, then **you play Abigail** on the gift
run down the mountain. The boss is an anger meter that her gifts and words bring down.

## The mission (a proposal: Glen decides)
**Half one, David: "a wall to them by night and by day" (25:16).** Nabal's shepherds are out in the fields of Carmel
with 3,000 sheep (25:2). David and his men guard them. Wolves, jackals, a lion and a bear come for the flock, and
lambs wander off. David drives off the animals and carries the strays back to the pen (Part One's lamb carry, with the
sprites already here). The shepherds count the flock at the end of each day. **Nothing missing** (25:7, 25:15) is the
goal: every sheep home before night.
- Day, then night: the same fields again in the dark (25:16, "by night and by day"), with the lamp.
- When the flock is safe, it's shearing time. David sends ten young men to Nabal (25:5-8).

**The turn: the insult (25:10-13).** At the shearing feast, Nabal: "Who is David? Who is the son of Jesse?" The young
men bring the words back. David: "Every man put on his sword!" 400 men march. **The anger meter** fills on screen.

**Half two, Abigail (25:14-20).** A young shepherd runs to tell her. You play Abigail:
1. **Load the donkeys:** gather the gifts around Nabal's house and storerooms (loaves, raisins, fig cakes, parched
   grain), quickly and without Nabal seeing (25:19, "she didn't tell her husband").
2. **The ride down the mountain** (25:20, "came down hidden by the mountain"): Abigail's donkey is this world's item
   (ITEMS_PLAN.md: fast, jumps wide gaps). A downhill run on the donkey, against the clock: David's band comes down the
   other side, and the anger meter keeps rising while she rides.
3. **The meeting: the boss.** She gets off and bows (25:23). Each gift given and each of her words (25:24-31) brings
   the meter down. Her words could be choices: the true, humble ones bring it down; the meter only empties when she
   has said them all.
4. **David turns back** (25:32-35). "Blessed is your discretion..."

**The end.** Nabal at his feast; told simply (25:37-38, or left out). Then 25:39-42: David sends for Abigail, and she
rides out with her five maids to become his wife. The Journal page; Psalm 57:7, "My heart is steadfast."

## The story beats (1 Samuel 25, WEB from ebible.org/eng-web, checked 2026-11-20; the game says "the LORD" for "Yahweh")
- 25:1 Samuel died; and all Israel gathered themselves together and mourned for him, and buried him at his house at Ramah. Then David arose and went down to the wilderness of Paran.
- 25:2 There was a man in Maon whose possessions were in Carmel; and the man was very great. He had three thousand sheep and a thousand goats; and he was shearing his sheep in Carmel.
- 25:3 Now the name of the man was Nabal; and the name of his wife Abigail. This woman was intelligent and had a beautiful face; but the man was surly and evil in his doings.
- 25:6 "Long life to you! Peace be to you! Peace be to your house! Peace be to all that you have!"
- 25:7 Your shepherds have now been with us, and we didn't harm them. Nothing was missing from them all the time they were in Carmel.
- 25:10 Nabal answered David's servants and said, "Who is David? Who is the son of Jesse? There are many servants who break away from their masters these days."
- 25:13 David said to his men, "Every man put on his sword!" ... About four hundred men followed David, and two hundred stayed by the baggage.
- 25:15 But the men were very good to us, and we were not harmed, and we didn't miss anything as long as we went with them, when we were in the fields.
- 25:16 They were a wall to us both by night and by day, all the while we were with them keeping the sheep.
- 25:18 Then Abigail hurried and took two hundred loaves of bread, two containers of wine, five sheep ready dressed, five seahs of parched grain, one hundred clusters of raisins, and two hundred cakes of figs, and laid them on donkeys.
- 25:19 She said to her young men, "Go on before me. Behold, I am coming after you." But she didn't tell her husband, Nabal.
- 25:20 As she rode on her donkey, and came down hidden by the mountain, behold, David and his men came down toward her, and she met them.
- 25:23 When Abigail saw David, she hurried and got off her donkey, and fell before David on her face and bowed herself to the ground.
- 25:24 She fell at his feet and said, "On me, my lord, on me be the blame! Please let your servant speak in your ears. Hear the words of your servant."
- 25:28 Please forgive the trespass of your servant. For the LORD will certainly make my lord a sure house, because my lord fights the LORD's battles. Evil will not be found in you all your days.
- 25:29 Though men may rise up to pursue you and to seek your soul, yet the soul of my lord will be bound in the bundle of life with the LORD your God. He will sling out the souls of your enemies as from a sling's pocket.
- 25:32 David said to Abigail, "Blessed is the LORD, the God of Israel, who sent you today to meet me!"
- 25:33 Blessed is your discretion, and blessed are you, who have kept me today from blood guiltiness, and from avenging myself with my own hand.
- 25:35 So David received from her hand that which she had brought him. Then he said to her, "Go up in peace to your house. Behold, I have listened to your voice and have granted your request."
- 25:37 In the morning, when the wine had gone out of Nabal, his wife told him these things; and his heart died within him, and he became as a stone.
- 25:38 About ten days later, the LORD struck Nabal, so that he died.
- 25:42 Abigail hurriedly arose and rode on a donkey with her five maids who followed her; and she went after the messengers of David, and became his wife.
- Kid-clean: 25:22 and 25:34 (the "wall" line) are left out. The wine in 25:18 and 25:36-37 can stay in the verse text
  but isn't a gift you carry; the gifts are loaves, raisins, fig cakes and grain.
- The Truth: 25:29, "the bundle of life" and the sling's pocket (David's own sling, in Abigail's words).
- Psalm 57:7: "My heart is steadfast, God. My heart is steadfast. I will sing, yes, I will sing praises." (as START_HERE.md has it)
