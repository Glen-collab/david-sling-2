# World 2 (The Window): prompts for the room paintings

Same look as Gibeah: warm, painterly, storybook 3D style, ancient Israel around 1000 BC. Every prompt ends with the
same tail, so the rooms match each other:

> *Side-scrolling 2D game background, seen straight on from the side, wide 16:9, no people, no animals, no text,
> nothing in the bottom eighth (the ground is drawn by the game).*

Save the results in `david-sling-2-art/rooms/ramah/` and tell me the names. Night rooms: deep blue moonlight, warm orange
torchlight in a few spots, still readable (not black).

## Start with these two (they're the new kinds of room)
1. **The palace wall at night (tall).** The outside of Saul's stone palace at Gibeah at night, seen from outside:
   a sheer wall of big pale limestone blocks, a high window with a wooden shutter, a few small torch-lit windows,
   moonlight across the stones, the town's rooftops far below. *Make it tall: a vertical strip, 9:16.*
   (Like your tower wall art, but night, so a seamless tile of the wall would work too.)
2. **Gibeah's rooftops at night.** Flat mud-brick rooftops of a hilltop town at night, stepped at different heights,
   low parapets, ladders between roofs, drying flax and clay jars on the roofs, a few torches on poles, the palace
   tower dark against a starry sky.

## The rest
3. **The night road to Ramah.** A rocky hill road through Benjamin at night, olive trees and boulders, the sky
   just starting to pale at the horizon (blue going to grey-pink), a far-off village light on a hill.
4. **The great well at Secu (tall).** Inside a huge ancient stone well shaft, looking at its side wall: rough cut
   rock, wooden beams across it, rope-worn grooves, a little moss, light from far above fading to dark blue water
   at the bottom. *Vertical strip, 9:16.*
5. **Ramah.** Samuel's hill town in early morning: small stone houses, an olive press yard, grapevines on trellises,
   a peaceful safe place, soft gold light.
6. **Naioth, the prophets' house (tall).** Inside a large ancient stone hall where prophets study: tall wooden
   shelves of clay jars holding scrolls, oil lamps in niches, wooden walkways along the walls at several heights,
   warm lamplight, a quiet holy feeling. *Vertical strip, 9:16.*
7. **The New Moon feast hall.** Saul's banquet hall at night: a long low table with bread, cups and lamps, cushions,
   pillars, woven hangings, one empty seat, a spear leaning on the wall behind the king's seat, tense dim light.
8. **The field of the arrows.** A wide field outside a hilltop town in the morning, grain stubble and rocks, one
   large standing stone with a hollow under it (the stone Ezel), the town's walls far off on the hill.
